Privacy

This site runs no analytics and loads nothing from other servers. It sets one cookie, and only after you accept.

What the server sees

Cloudflare serves the pages and keeps standard request logs (address, time, page) for security, as its own policy describes.

The app

Apotheke runs on your own server. Your catalogue never passes through this site.

Cookies

The notice asks before anything is stored. Decline stores nothing, and the notice comes back on the next visit. Accept sets one cookie.

  • Nameapotheke_consent
  • Valuev1. Any other value is not consent.
  • PurposeIt remembers that you accepted.
  • Lifetime180 days.
  • DomainOn getapotheke.com it is set for that domain, so the browser sends it to every getapotheke.com subdomain. api.services.getapotheke.com reads it as consent. autofill.services.getapotheke.com stores nothing from it. On any other host the cookie is host-only.
