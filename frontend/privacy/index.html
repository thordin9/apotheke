Privacy

This site loads nothing from other servers. Only after you accept, it sets one cookie and counts page views without cookies.

What the server sees

Cloudflare serves the pages and keeps standard request logs (address, time, page) for security, as its own policy describes.

Page views

After you accept, each page view is counted with Counterscale, which this project runs on its own Cloudflare account at analytics.services.webapp.gr. Its script is part of this site. Before you accept, or after you decline, it is not loaded and nothing is sent.

  • RecordedThe page (its path, without the query string), the site you came from, any campaign (utm_) tags in the address, your browser's user-agent string and what is read from it (browser, its major version, device type and model), and your country, which Cloudflare derives from your address. Your address itself is not stored.
  • Stored on your deviceNothing: no cookie, no local storage. A repeat visit on the same day is recognised through your browser's ordinary cache (a date the counter returns, which your browser sends back).
  • Where and how longCloudflare Workers Analytics Engine, which deletes each record after 90 days. If daily roll-ups are turned on, they are kept longer in Cloudflare R2: one count of views and visitors per day for each combination of the fields above, without the time of day.
  • Who sees itOnly the project maintainer, behind a sign-in.

The app

Apotheke runs on your own server. Your catalogue never passes through this site.

Cookies

The notice asks before anything is stored or counted. Decline stores nothing and counts nothing, and the notice comes back on the next visit. Accept sets one cookie and turns on page-view counting.

  • Nameapotheke_consent
  • Valuev1. Any other value is not consent.
  • PurposeIt remembers that you accepted.
  • Lifetime180 days.
  • DomainOn getapotheke.com it is set for that domain, so the browser sends it to every getapotheke.com subdomain. api.services.getapotheke.com reads it as consent. autofill.services.getapotheke.com stores nothing from it. On any other host the cookie is host-only.

Withdrawing consent

Delete the apotheke_consent cookie for getapotheke.com in your browser's settings (site data or cookies). From the next page you open, nothing more is counted and the notice asks again. Views counted before you withdrew stay in the totals until they expire.
