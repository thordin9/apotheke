## Findings

- **L-2533:** as declared, the downgrade and override checks read the plan outside the batch that writes, so a concurrent plan change can slip between them. Rare with a handful of operators; documented. Revisit with a conditional write if the console gets more users.
- **L-2534 (closed at `c8cd5ac`):** the new routes sit behind #352's `writes_disabled` gate, tested per route and value. The gate lives only in `route()`, which every request passes through.
- **N-2535 (closed at `51ba50e`):** the by-id `primary` refusal is tested with no primary row present.
- **N-2536 (closed at `51ba50e`):** confirming a purge before retention is over is 409 `retention`; the job stays paused, with no audit row and no Workflow call.
- **N-2537 (closed at `51ba50e`):** `jobAudit` is exported (its only source change) and its credential refusal is tested.
- **N-2538:** as declared, the subscription upsert has no optimistic-concurrency check, and the purge retention rule is a copy of `lifecycle.ts` (identical today). Lowering a plan's limits also has no downgrade guard, unlike a subscription change, and touches every installation on that plan.

0 High, 0 Medium, 1 open Low (L-2533) and 1 open Nit (N-2538); L-2534 and N-2535 to N-2537 are closed.
